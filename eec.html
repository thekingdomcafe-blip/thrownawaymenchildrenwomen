ILLINOIS ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT CENTER

Building stronger entrepreneurs and small businesses.

A proposed Harvey-based Economic Empowerment Center focused on underserved entrepreneurs and very small businesses.

Services

  • One-on-one business advising
  • Business concept and feasibility development
  • Market analysis and customer discovery
  • Business planning and financial management
  • Financial literacy and capital readiness
  • Marketing, sales, and digital business education
  • Licensing and resource navigation
  • Procurement and growth strategy
  • Partner and community referrals
  • Graduate technical assistance and follow-up

How success will be measured

Planned measures include participant intake, advising sessions, workshops, business plans, businesses launched, businesses stabilized or expanded, financing applications and financing obtained, referrals, jobs created or retained, and participant satisfaction.
