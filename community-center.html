Our community center project

Transforming 175 E. 154th Street into a place for community.

A proposed Community Resource Center in Harvey designed to bring outreach, prevention, mentoring, family engagement, and resource connections together in one welcoming location.

Support the campaign

Why this center matters

Families and individuals often face multiple barriers at once. A neighborhood-based center can help make information, relationships, prevention activities, and connections to community resources easier to reach.

The project focuses on rehabilitating and preparing the property for community-serving use.

PROJECT LOCATION

175 E. 154th Street

Harvey, Illinois

THROWN AWAY MEN CHILDREN WOMEN NFP is developing this property as a community resource and opportunity center.

Planned community uses

Outreach & prevention

Community meetings, prevention education, neighborhood engagement, and resource information.

Youth & family connection

Mentoring activities, family engagement, educational opportunities, and positive community gatherings.

Resource navigation

Information, referrals, and connections to housing, reentry, recovery, and other support providers.

Contributions to the campaign support the transformation of the property and its preparation for community use. See the campaign page for current fundraising information and donation options.
