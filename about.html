About the organization

Building pathways to safety, belonging, and self-sufficiency.

THROWN AWAY MEN CHILDREN WOMEN NFP is a community-based nonprofit serving children, women, and men through outreach, prevention, mentoring, education, partnerships, and supportive resource connections.

Our mission

We connect children, women, and men with supportive resources, education, community-based programs, and pathways that promote safety, belonging, and self-sufficiency.

Our approach

Through partnerships and community-driven initiatives, we work to address barriers, build stronger relationships, and create healthier, more connected communities.

Who we serve

Our mission includes the whole family and community: men, women, children, youth, caregivers, and individuals navigating difficult transitions or barriers to stability.

Our guiding principles

Dignity

Meet people with respect and recognize each person's strengths and circumstances.

Prevention

Share education, build protective connections, and strengthen community support.

Partnership

Work alongside residents and qualified organizations to connect people with appropriate services.
